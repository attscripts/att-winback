AT&T Business Fiber — Agent Script

GOLDEN RULE: Lead with what THEY save. Never claim they were an AT&T customer — you don't know that. Be the helpful pro who found them a better deal before it expires.
STEP 1

Opening — reach the decision-maker

Friendly, confident, fast. Get to the owner/manager.

Hi, good morning — this is [AGENT] calling from AT&T Business Fiber Solutions. Am I speaking with the owner or whoever handles the internet and phone?
IF YESContinue to Step 2.
IF GATEKEEPER"It's about lowering the business's internet and phone bill — the owner will want to hear it. Can you put me through for a minute?"
STEP 2

Why you're calling — the 5-minute ask

Lead with their savings. Low pressure. Just ask for 5 minutes.

Quick reason for my call — we're helping local businesses lower their internet and phone bill. All I need is about 5 minutes to see if we can cut what you're spending. If I can't save you money, I'll let you go — no problem. Fair enough?
STEP 3

Discover — make it about THEIR savings

This one question qualifies them AND gives you the savings angle.

Quick question so I don't waste your time — who do you use for internet right now, and roughly what are you paying a month?
PAYS A LOT / UNHAPPYGold. "Perfect — I can almost certainly beat that AND get you a reward card. Let me show you." → Step 4.
HAPPY / CHEAP FIBER"Totally fair." Try the reward-card angle once. If still no → thank them, move to next call. Don't force a bad fit.
STEP 4

The offer — the hook

Say it with energy. The reward card is the star.

Here's what's on the table with AT&T Business Fiber right now: faster speed, usually a lower bill than you've got, no contract, plus a Visa reward card from $300 up to $500 just for switching, and free professional install. If you're under contract elsewhere, AT&T will buy it out up to $750. You can also add business phone lines at $15 each.
That reward card basically covers your first few months of service. Honestly, there's no reason not to switch.
And the reason I'm reaching you now instead of later — the promo and the reward card end June 30th.
STEP 5

Compliance — mandatory

MUST READ BEFORE CONTINUING "Before we go further, I need to let you know this call is recorded for quality and verification."
STEP 6

Soft close — assume the sale

Don't ask "do you want it." Ask when. Then offer add-ons.

All I need is your OK to get you set up at that lower price with the reward card. What day works best for the technician to come out?
Want me to add Business Phone at $15/line, or Dynamic Defense internet security — starts at $15/mo with 2 months free?
IF YESConfirm the add-on, note it, continue to details.
IF NONo problem — continue to details.
STEP 7

Capture the details

Read back each one to confirm spelling. Especially the email.

  • Business legal name
  • Contact name + title
  • Service address (must be fiber-served — verify)
  • Best callback number
  • Email (read it back letter by letter)
  • Current provider + monthly bill
  • Speed chosen + add-ons
STEP 8

Recap, disclosure & close

Read clearly — do not rush this part.

Let me recap: you're getting AT&T Business Fiber [SPEED] at $[PRICE]/mo, a $[CARD] reward card, free install, and no contract.
REWARD CARD DISCLOSURE — REQUIRED "Important: you'll get a postcard mailed to your billing address. You must complete the redemption with it to receive the reward card — it arrives a few weeks after activation."
The technician will confirm any extra wiring before starting — no surprises. Someone 18 or older must be on site with access to where the service goes in.
Last step — I'll email you a Letter of Authorization. Please sign it and email it back so we can lock in your order and your reward card.
Welcome to AT&T Business, and thank you for your time today. Have a great one!

🔁 Rebuttals — handle, don't argue

"I'm not interested."
"Totally understand — most people say that before they hear the number. All I'm really offering is a lower bill and up to $500 back. Worth 30 seconds?"
"I already have internet."
"Perfect — that's exactly who this is for. Who are you with and what are you paying? If I can't beat it, I'll tell you straight."
"I'm happy with my provider."
"Glad it's working. The difference here is the $300–$500 reward card and no contract — even happy customers don't leave money on the table. Want me to check your address?"
"How much is it?"
"Depends on the speed, but most businesses land lower than they pay now — and the reward card covers your first few months. What are you paying today so I can compare?"
"Send me information / email me."
"Happy to — but the reward card ends June 30th, so let me check your address live and at least lock the offer. Takes one minute. What's the business address?"
"I don't have time."
"I hear you — one minute now could save you a few hundred a year. When's a better time today, this afternoon or tomorrow morning?"
"Is this a contract?"
"No contract at all. And if you're locked in elsewhere, AT&T buys it out up to $750."

Sales Script · Cold Outbound · Business Internet & Phone
⚠ Fill ALL required fields before submitting. No field should be left empty.

Opening & Gatekeeper

Phase 1 · Reach the person who handles the Internet account

📋 Product Quick Facts — Know Before You Dial

  • Selling: AT&T Business Internet & Phone — brand new offer in their area
  • Speeds: 1Gig×1Gig · 300Mb×300Mb · 100Mb×100Mb — price $___ /mo (TBD — confirm with AT&T)
  • No contract · No termination fees · No overage charges
  • Fastest business package — switch made in 5 business days
  • Dedicated AT&T customer service & support team
  • Install: $99 full tech install — refunded after 3 on-time payments
  • Add-ons: Wireless Internet Backup $15/mo OR 5 Static IPs for $15
  • Target: Cold outbound to local businesses (Google Maps lists)
Say
Hi, this is [AGENT NAME] calling on behalf of AT&T Business Internet and Phone Services.
Ask · Gatekeeper
I'm trying to reach the person who handles your Internet account — are they available?
COMPLIANCE: The recorded line disclosure is MANDATORY. Do NOT skip this. It must be stated clearly before continuing the call.
TIP: This is cold outbound from a Google Maps list — expect gatekeepers. Ask for the owner or office manager by role, stay upbeat, and don't pitch the gatekeeper. Once you reach the decision-maker and the recorded line disclosure is done, go to Phase 2.

The Pitch — Business Internet & Phone

Phase 2 · New offer + speed comparison + key differentiators
Say
We wanted to give you this important information on our brand new offer for business internet and phone service in this area.
Say · Comparison
Let me give you a quick comparison — we offer:
• 1 Gig × 1 Gig for $___ /mo
• 300 Mb × 300 Mb for $___ /mo
• 100 Mb × 100 Mb for $___ /mo
Say · Differentiators
There's no contract, no termination fees, and no overage charges. This is our fastest business package, and we can make the change in just 5 business days for you.
Say · Support
The offer includes dedicated customer service and support — AT&T built this team of professionals to give small and mid-sized businesses like yours the best possible care.
TIP: Lead with the speed tier that fits their business size. Let "faster, lower price, no contract" land before you ask for the approval in Phase 3.

Soft Close & Add-Ons

Phase 3 · Get approval + book the tech + offer backup / static IPs
Ask · Soft Close
All we need is your approval so we can replace your current circuit with a faster one at a lower price. What would be the best time for a technician to come to your business?
TIP: This is an assumptive close — ask for the install time, not "do you want it." If they hesitate or object → go to Phase 7 (Objections), then come back here.
Say · Add-On Offer
One last thing — I'd also recommend AT&T Wireless Internet Backup for only $15/mo, or 5 Static IPs for $15 more.
Ask
Would you like to add the Wireless Internet Backup or the Static IPs?
IF YES Confirm which add-on (Backup $15/mo OR 5 Static IPs $15) and log it in the form panel. Then continue to Phase 4 (Order Details).
IF NO No problem — mark "No" on both add-ons in the form and continue to Phase 4 (Order Details).

Order Details

Phase 4 · Capture business information — fill the form panel as you go →
Transition
Great! To get your order started, I just need to confirm a few quick details about your business.
  1. Can I get the proper spelling of your first and last name?
  2. What is your legal business name?
  3. Is your business a Corporation, LLC, or Sole Proprietor?
  4. What is the business number?
  5. What is the best number to reach you at?
  6. Secondary contact person (used at time of install)?
  7. Secondary contact phone number (used at time of install)?
  8. What is the full service address?
  9. What is the best time to reach you?
  10. Which speed would you like? (1Gig / 300Mb / 100Mb)
  11. Static IP addresses requested?
  12. Internet Backup requested?
  13. What is your EIN? That's your tax ID number — it identifies your business in our system.
TIP: Capture each answer in the form panel on the right as you go. Do NOT wait until the end of the call — type as the customer speaks.

Recap & Compliance Disclosure

Phase 5 · Required disclosure — read clearly, do not rush
READ WORD-FOR-WORD. Do not paraphrase. AT&T's regulatory and legal disclosure requirements depend on the exact wording below.
Say · Recap
Let me recap: we'll replace your current circuit as a new order with [SPEED] at a lower price of $[PRICE] /mo.
Say · Install Fee
There is a $99 fee for a full tech install. This is refunded in 3 months — once you pay on time for the 3 months, this is a standard refund.
Say · No Surprises
At the time of your installation, the technician will inform you of any additional wiring or charges before beginning your installation, if any — so there are no surprises. We do guarantee service once it is in and working.
Say · Access Requirement
You, or someone you designate who is 18 years of age or older, must be present during the entire install and provide "free and ready" access to any outlet where service will be installed.
Confirm the EIN / Tax ID has been captured before moving on. If not, ask for it now.

Confirmation & Close

Phase 6 · Letter of Authorization + strong close
Say · Letter of Authorization
I'm going to email you a Letter of Authorization. Please sign it and email it back so we can finalize your order.
Say · Polite Close
Thank you for choosing AT&T and for your time today. Have a great day!
TIP: Read the email address back out loud to confirm spelling — the LOA goes there. The order is NOT final until the signed LOA comes back. Set a follow-up if you don't get it same-day.
Once the call is wrapped, submit the form on the right → Phase 8 has the final compliance checklist.

Objection Handling

Phase 7 · Use these responses when businesses push back
ObjectionRecommended Response
"I'm under contract with my current provider."That's fine — we have no contract and no termination fees on our side, and we can time the switch for when your current term ends. Many businesses save enough on the lower monthly price that it more than covers any early-term cost. Let's lock in today's offer so it's ready when you are.
"I'm happy with my current provider."Glad it's working for you. The difference here is faster speed at a lower price with no contract — and we make the switch in 5 business days with zero overage charges. It's worth a few minutes to cut your monthly bill and boost your speed.
"Is this really AT&T?"Yes — we're an authorized AT&T Solutions Provider for business internet and phone. The order goes directly onto AT&T's network, and you get AT&T billing and AT&T's dedicated business support team.
"Just email me the information."Happy to email you the Letter of Authorization. But the pricing is location-based, and I can lock in today's offer for you right now in about two minutes — then email the confirmation. What speed fits your business best?
"I don't have time right now."Completely understand — this only takes a couple of minutes and could lower your monthly bill. When's a better time today for a quick callback so I can hold this offer for you?
"I had a bad experience with AT&T before."I hear you. This is a dedicated business team built specifically to fix that — a direct support line and no-contract terms, so you're never locked in. If the service ever falls short, you can leave with no termination fee.
"This sounds too good to be true — what's the catch?"No catch: no contract, no termination fees, faster speed, lower price. The only fee is the $99 full tech install, and that's fully refunded after 3 on-time payments.
"I don't want a technician at my business."The install is quick, and the tech will tell you about any extra wiring or charges before starting — no surprises. You just need someone 18 or older on site with access, and we schedule it at your convenience within 5 business days.
"I need to check with my partner / IT person."Absolutely — who handles that, and what's the best number? I can also send the Letter of Authorization so they can review the exact terms. Let's pencil in a quick callback so you don't lose today's pricing.
"How much exactly?"Pricing depends on the speed you choose and your location. If you confirm your service address, I can give you the exact monthly price for each speed tier right now.

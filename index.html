Opening

Phase 1 · Reach the owner/manager · lead with the offer · ask for the quote
Say · Opener
Hi, this is [AGENT NAME] with AT&T Business Fiber. We have fiber available in your area, and I wanted to speak with the manager or owner, please.
Say · The Hook
The reason for my call — we've got faster speeds, lower prices, and no contract. Right now I can get your business 300 Megs at about $70/mo with Free Tech Install. You can also add Phone Service for Business at $15/line. This is the best offer we have for you.
Say · The Money Line
You get free install — and if you're under contract elsewhere, we'll buy it out up to $750. Faster speed at a lower price, with no contract.
Say · Reason for the Call
That's the reason we wanted to talk to your business — maybe send you a quote and come to an agreement.
COMPLIANCE: State the recorded-line disclosure before continuing. Mandatory.

Recap & Compliance Disclosure

Phase 5 · Required disclosure — read clearly, do not rush
READ CLEARLY. Confirm the key terms so there are no surprises and the sale holds.

Submit the form on the right. The order is NOT final until the signed LOA comes back — set a follow-up if not received same-day. See the Checklist (page 9) before submitting.

Tips & Rebuttals

Reference · Pull these up when you need them — not read out loud

⚡ Quick Tips

  • Lead with faster speed + lower price + no contract — that's the hook now.
  • Lead with the speed that fits their size — don't list all tiers, pick one.
  • Assumptive close: ask when the tech comes, not if they want it.
  • Type as they talk — fill the form panel live, don't wait till the end.
  • Use the value: free install + up to $750 ETF buyout is your urgency every call.
  • Confirm the email for the LOA — no signed LOA, no sale.
ObjectionRebuttal
"I'm under contract with my current provider."That's fine — we have no contract and no termination fees on our side, and we can time the switch for when your current term ends. Many businesses save enough on the lower monthly price that it more than covers any early-term cost. Let's lock in today's offer so it's ready when you are.
"I'm happy with my current provider."Glad it's working for you. The difference is faster speed at a lower price, no contract, plus free install and we'll buy out your current contract up to $750. Worth a couple minutes to cut your monthly bill.
"Is this really AT&T?"Yes — we're an authorized AT&T Solutions Provider for business internet and phone. The order goes directly onto AT&T's network, and you get AT&T billing and AT&T's dedicated business support team.
"Just email me the information."Happy to email you the Letter of Authorization. But the pricing is location-based, and I can lock in today's offer for you right now in about two minutes — then email the confirmation. What speed fits your business best?
"I don't have time right now."Completely understand — this only takes a couple of minutes and could lower your monthly bill. When's a better time today for a quick callback so I can hold this offer for you?
"I had a bad experience with AT&T before."I hear you. This is a dedicated business team built specifically to fix that — a direct support line and no-contract terms, so you're never locked in. If the service ever falls short, you can leave with no termination fee.
"This sounds too good to be true — what's the catch?"No catch: no contract, no termination fees, free install, faster speed, and a lower monthly price — plus we'll buy out your current contract up to $750. It's AT&T's strongest business offer right now.
"I don't want a technician at my business."The install is quick and free, and the tech tells you about any extra wiring before starting — no surprises. You just need someone 18 or older on site with access, scheduled at your convenience.
"I need to check with my partner / IT person."Absolutely — who handles that, and what's the best number? I can also send the Letter of Authorization so they can review the exact terms. Let's pencil in a quick callback so you don't lose today's pricing.
"Let me think about it / call me later."I understand — the free install and up to $750 ETF buyout are some of the strongest offers we have, and pricing is locked to today. Let's at least lock in your eligibility now so you don't lose it — it only takes a couple minutes.
"How much exactly?"Pricing depends on the speed you choose and your location. If you confirm your service address, I can give you the exact monthly price for each speed tier right now.

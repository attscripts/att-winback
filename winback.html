Opening & Gatekeeper

Phase 1 · Reach the person who handles the Internet account

📋 Cheat Sheet — Know Before You Dial

  • Selling: AT&T Business Fiber (winback / switch)
  • 🔥 JUNE PROMO (ends 6/30) — reward card by speed: 300M → $300 · 500M → $400 · 1G/2G/5G → $500
  • FREE install · No contract · ETF buyout up to $750 if switching
  • Price (approx): 300M ~$60 · 500M ~$100 · 1G ~$140 — as low as ~$30 converged w/ wireless
  • 1Gig+ includes 5G backup · Add-ons: Phone $15/line · Dynamic Defense from $15/mo
  • ⚠ Confirm exact price by address in the AT&T portal before quoting.
Say
Hi, this is [AGENT NAME] calling on behalf of AT&T Business Fiber.
Ask · Gatekeeper
I'm trying to reach the person who handles your Internet account — are they available?
COMPLIANCE: The recorded line disclosure is MANDATORY. Do NOT skip this. It must be stated clearly before continuing the call.
TIP: This is cold outbound from a Google Maps list — expect gatekeepers. Ask for the owner or office manager by role, stay upbeat, and don't pitch the gatekeeper. Once you reach the decision-maker and the recorded line disclosure is done, go to Phase 2.

The Pitch

Phase 2 · Offer + reward card. Keep it short — let them talk.
Say · Hook
We've got a limited-time offer on AT&T Business Fiber in your area — faster speed, lower price, no contract, and a cash reward card just for switching. And it ends June 30th.
Say · Speed + Reward (pick the tier that fits them)
• 300 Mbps → ~$60/mo · $300 reward card
• 500 Mbps → ~$100/mo · $400 reward card
• 1 Gig → ~$140/mo · $500 reward card · incl. 5G backup
Add AT&T Business Wireless and fiber drops as low as ~$30/mo.
Say · The Money Line
That reward card basically covers your first 3–4 months free. Plus free install, and if you're under contract elsewhere we'll buy it out up to $750. There's really no reason not to come back to AT&T.
TIP: Always say price + reward card together: "500 Meg is about $100/mo AND you get $400 back." The card is your #1 hook. You can add Phone for Business at $15/line if they need voice.

Soft Close & Add-Ons

Phase 3 · Assume the sale — book the install
Ask · Soft Close
All I need is your OK to get you set up at that lower price with the reward card. What day works best for the technician to come out?
TIP: Assumptive close — ask when, not if. If they hesitate → Phase 7 (Objections), then come back.
Say · Add-Ons (optional)
Want me to add Phone for Business at $15/line, or Dynamic Defense security (starts at $15/mo, often 2 months free)? Up to you.
IF YESConfirm the add-on and log it in the form. Continue to Phase 4.
IF NONo problem — continue to Phase 4.

Recap & Compliance Disclosure

Phase 5 · Required disclosure — read clearly, do not rush
READ CLEARLY. Confirm the key terms so there are no surprises and the sale holds.
Say · Recap
Let me recap: you're getting AT&T Business Fiber [SPEED] at $[PRICE]/mo, with a $[CARD] reward card, free install, and no contract.
Say · Reward Card Redemption
Important on the reward card: you'll get a postcard mailed to your billing address — you must complete the redemption with it to receive the card. It arrives a few weeks after activation.
Say · Install Access
The technician will confirm any extra wiring before starting — no surprises. Someone 18 or older must be on site with access to where the service goes in. Service is guaranteed once it's working.
Confirm EIN / Tax ID is captured before moving on. If switching, note the ETF buyout (up to $750) needs their final bill from the old carrier.

Confirmation & Close

Phase 6 · Letter of Authorization + strong close
Say · Letter of Authorization
I'm going to email you a Letter of Authorization. Please sign it and email it back so we can finalize your order.
Say · Polite Close
Thank you for choosing AT&T and for your time today. Have a great day!
TIP: Read the email address back out loud to confirm spelling — the LOA goes there. The order is NOT final until the signed LOA comes back. Set a follow-up if you don't get it same-day.
Once the call is wrapped, submit the form on the right → Phase 8 has the final compliance checklist.

Objection Handling

Phase 7 · Use these responses when businesses push back
ObjectionRecommended Response
"I'm under contract with my current provider."That's fine — we have no contract and no termination fees on our side, and we can time the switch for when your current term ends. Many businesses save enough on the lower monthly price that it more than covers any early-term cost. Let's lock in today's offer so it's ready when you are.
"I'm happy with my current provider."Glad it's working for you. The difference here is faster speed at a lower price with no contract — and we make the switch in 5 business days with zero overage charges. It's worth a few minutes to cut your monthly bill and boost your speed.
"Is this really AT&T?"Yes — we're an authorized AT&T Solutions Provider for business internet and phone. The order goes directly onto AT&T's network, and you get AT&T billing and AT&T's dedicated business support team.
"Just email me the information."Happy to email you the Letter of Authorization. But the pricing is location-based, and I can lock in today's offer for you right now in about two minutes — then email the confirmation. What speed fits your business best?
"I don't have time right now."Completely understand — this only takes a couple of minutes and could lower your monthly bill. When's a better time today for a quick callback so I can hold this offer for you?
"I had a bad experience with AT&T before."I hear you. This is a dedicated business team built specifically to fix that — a direct support line and no-contract terms, so you're never locked in. If the service ever falls short, you can leave with no termination fee.
"This sounds too good to be true — what's the catch?"No catch: no contract, no termination fees, faster speed, lower price. The only fee is the $99 full tech install, and that's fully refunded after 3 on-time payments.
"I don't want a technician at my business."The install is quick, and the tech will tell you about any extra wiring or charges before starting — no surprises. You just need someone 18 or older on site with access, and we schedule it at your convenience within 5 business days.
"I need to check with my partner / IT person."Absolutely — who handles that, and what's the best number? I can also send the Letter of Authorization so they can review the exact terms. Let's pencil in a quick callback so you don't lose today's pricing.
"Let me think about it / call me later."I understand — but this reward card promo ends June 30th. The $400–$500 card, free install, and ETF buyout are the strongest offers of the year and they go away after the 30th. Let's at least lock in your eligibility today so you don't lose it — it only takes a couple minutes.
"How much exactly?"Pricing depends on the speed you choose and your location. If you confirm your service address, I can give you the exact monthly price for each speed tier right now.
